Phase 0: Silver Age Foundations

1961 – 1970

🌟 The Birth of Marvel

The birth of the Marvel Universe. Stan Lee and Jack Kirby revolutionized comics with the Fantastic Four. Spider-Man and the X-Men follow. This is where it all begins. Read these in order to understand the foundation of the Marvel Universe.

📖 Reading Tracks

🔵 Fantastic Four Foundation

Fantastic Four #1-102
1961-1971 (Lee/Kirby)
The very first Silver Age title. Features the Galactus Trilogy (#48-50), the Inhumans (#43-48), and the birth of the Marvel Universe.

🕷️ Spider-Man Track

Amazing Spider-Man #1-38
1963-1966 (Lee/Ditko)
The birth of Spider-Man. Covers his origin, the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, and establishes him as a loner struggling with responsibility.

🔨 Thor Foundation

Journey Into Mystery / Thor #83-125
1962-1966 (Lee/Kirby)
The birth of Thor. Covers his origin, Loki's first schemes, and his early battles with frost giants and other cosmic threats.

⚡ Avengers Foundation

The Avengers #1-16
1963-1965 (Lee/Kirby)
The first team-up. Loki assembles the Avengers. Introduction of the team dynamics that define the Marvel Universe.

🔥 X-Men Foundation

The X-Men #1-43
1963-1968 (Lee/Kirby)
The birth of the X-Men. Covers their origin with Professor X, the introduction of Magneto, and the foundation of the anti-mutant prejudice themes.

🌀 Doctor Strange Foundation

Strange Tales #110-150
1963-1966 (Lee/Ditko)
Doctor Strange's origin and early adventures. Introduces the mystic arts and the Mystic Realms to the Marvel Universe.

⚙️ Iron Man Foundation

Tales of Suspense #39-99
1963-1968 (Lee/Heck)
Iron Man's origin in Vietnam. Tony Stark becomes a hero through technology and engineering brilliance.

✨ Cosmic Foundation

Silver Surfer #1-18
1968-1970 (Lee/Buscema)
The Silver Surfer is introduced. First appearance of Galactus. The birth of cosmic Marvel.

👑 Cosmic Threats

Captain Marvel #25-34 & Marvel Feature #12
1968-1970 (Lee/Heck)
First appearances of Captain Marvel and Thanos. The beginning of the cosmic saga that will dominate Marvel.
