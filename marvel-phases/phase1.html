Marvel Phase 1: The Avengers Initiative

2008-2012 | Iron Man through The Avengers

Phase Overview

Marvel Phase 1 establishes the foundation of the Marvel Cinematic Universe. It introduces the Avengers roster individually before bringing them together for the climactic battle against Loki and the Chitauri invasion. This phase focuses on origin stories and setting up the overarching threat of Thanos.
