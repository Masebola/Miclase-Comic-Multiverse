Marvel Phase 3: The Infinity War

2016-2019 | Captain America: Civil War through Endgame

Phase Overview

Phase 3 is the culmination of the MCU's first era. It features the Avengers split in Civil War, the emergence of the Black Panther, Doctor Strange's introduction to the mystic arts, Spider-Man's debut, and finally the devastating Infinity War leading to Endgame where the Avengers attempt to undo Thanos's snap.
