Phase 6: Rebirth & Metal

Years: 2016 - 2018 Anchor Event: Dark Nights: Metal

Hope returns. Legacy is restored. But beneath reality, a dark multiverse waits - a nightmare realm where every fear becomes a Batman who wins.

0 / 0 completed

EVENT PAUSE - READ FIRST

Dark Nights: Metal (2017-18)

What it is: Batman discovers the Dark Multiverse - a realm of nightmare realities where every worst-case scenario exists. The Dark Knights (evil Batmen from nightmare worlds) invade.

Why it matters: Introduces the Dark Multiverse, Perpetua, and the Monitor/Anti-Monitor hierarchy. Shatters the Source Wall. Sets up the next 5 years of DC.

Core reading: Dark Nights: Metal #1-6, Dark Nights: Batman - The Dawnbreaker, The Red Death, The Murder Machine, The Drowned, The Merciless, The Devastator, The Batman Who Laughs.

CONCURRENT READING TRACKS

Bat-Family Track

🦇
  • Batman: I Am Gotham / I Am Suicide / I Am Bane (Batman #1-20, 2016-17) Tom King's run begins. Gotham Girl, Bane's return, Suicide Squad infiltration.
  • Batman: The War of Jokes and Riddles (Batman #25-32, 2017) Flashback to an early Batman case. Joker and Riddler go to war over who gets to kill Batman.
  • Batman: Rules of Engagement (Batman #33-37, 2017-18) Batman and Catwoman's engagement. The Bat-Family reacts.
  • Batman: The Wedding (Batman #50, 2018) Bruce and Selina's wedding day. Bane manipulates Selina into leaving him at the altar.
  • Nightwing: Better Than Batman (2016-17) Dick returns to Bludhaven. Raptor, a villain from Dick's circus past, appears.
  • Batgirl: Beyond Burnside (2016-17) Barbara travels Asia, faces her own doppelganger.
  • Batwoman by Marguerite Bennett (2017-18) Kate Kane faces her past and the Many Arms of Death.
  • Red Hood: Outlaw (2016-18) Jason Todd's ongoing series. Bizarro and Artemis join.
  • Batman and the Signal (2018) Duke Thomas gets his own series. The Signal operates in daylight.

Flash Family Track

⚡
  • The Flash: Lightning Strikes Twice (The Flash: Rebirth #1, then The Flash #1-8, 2016) Barry's Rebirth origin. Godspeed (August Heart) becomes a speedster villain.
  • The Flash: Speed of Darkness (The Flash #9-13, 2017) Shade returns. Wally West is back fully.
  • The Flash: Running Scared (The Flash #23-27, 2017) Reverse-Flash (Eobard Thawne) returns. Barry proposes to Iris.
  • The Flash: Negative (The Flash #28-32, 2017-18) Negative Flash. Meena Dhawan's return.
  • Titans: The Return of Wally West (2016) Wally reintegrates into the world. The Titans reform.

Lantern Track

💚
  • Hal Jordan and the Green Lantern Corps: Sinestro's Law (2016-17) Sinestro conquers the universe with his fear army. Hal and the Corps fight back.
  • Hal Jordan and the Green Lantern Corps: Bottled Light (2017) The Green Lantern Corps is trapped in a bottle by Brainiac.
  • Green Lanterns: Rage Planet (2016-17) Jessica Cruz and Simon Baz as Earth's Green Lanterns.
  • Green Lanterns: Phantom Lantern (2017) Frank Laminski, a man who wants to be a Lantern, finds the Phantom Ring.

Superman Track

🔴
  • Superman: Son of Superman (Superman: Rebirth #1, Superman #1-6, 2016) The pre-Flashpoint Superman raises his son Jon in the New 52 world.
  • Superman: Trials of the Super Son (Superman #10-13, 2017) Superman and Batman's sons (Jon and Damian) team up.
  • Superman: Multiplicity (Superman #14-17, 2017) Superman meets Superman from across the multiverse.
  • Superman: Black Dawn (Superman #20-26, 2017) Jon's powers emerge. The Kent farm hides dark secrets.
  • Superboy and Robin: Super Sons (2017-18) Jon Kent and Damian Wayne as a team. Comedy and heart.
  • Supergirl: Rebirth / Escape from the Phantom Zone (2016-17) Kara faces Cyborg Superman and the Phantom Zone.

Wonder Woman Track

⭐
  • Wonder Woman: The Lies / Year One (Wonder Woman: Rebirth #1, Wonder Woman #1-14, 2016) Greg Rucka's return. The New 52 origin is a lie. The true origin is told in parallel.
  • Wonder Woman: The Truth / Godwatch (Wonder Woman #15-24, 2017) Diana faces the truth about the gods. Cheetah's origin is redefined.

Titans / Justice League Track

🌊
  • Justice League: The Extinction Machine (2016-17) The League reforms. The Kindred, a psychic entity, attacks.
  • Justice League vs. Suicide Squad (2016-17) The League clashes with Amanda Waller's team.
  • Justice League: No Justice (2018) The Source Wall breaks. New cosmic teams form.
  • Titans: The Return of Wally West (2016) Wally West is back. The original Titans reunite.
  • Teen Titans by Benjamin Percy (2016-18) Damian Wayne leads the Teen Titans. A darker, more aggressive team.

Cosmic / New Gods Track

🌌
  • Dark Nights: Metal (2017-18) The Dark Multiverse. The Dark Knights. Barbatos.
  • Dark Nights: The Batman Who Laughs (2017) A Batman who killed the Joker and became him. The most dangerous being in the Dark Multiverse.

Magic / Vertigo Track

🔮
  • Justice League Dark: The Last Age of Magic (2018) Wonder Woman leads a new JLD. Hecate, goddess of magic, threatens reality.
  • Raven (2016-17) Raven's solo series. Trigon returns.
  • Trinity: Better Together (2016-18) Batman, Superman, Wonder Woman as a team. Magic and cosmic threats.

Archers / Street Track

🏹

  • Green Arrow: Emerald Outlaw (2016-17) Ollie is framed as a terrorist.
  • Green Arrow: The Rise of Star City (2017-18) The Ninth Circle (corporate villains) destroys Seattle.

Atlantis Track

🌊
  • Aquaman: The Drowning (2016) Aquaman's Rebirth. Black Manta attacks Atlantis.
  • Aquaman: Black Manta Rising (2017) Manta's war on Arthur continues.
  • Aquaman: Underworld (2017-18) Atlantis falls into civil war. Corum Rath usurps the throne.

Villain Track

💀
  • Batman: The War of Jokes and Riddles (2017) Joker vs. Riddler. A gang war that tears Gotham apart. Kite Man (hell yeah) is a casualty.
  • Batman: I Am Bane (2017) Bane returns to Gotham. He breaks Batman psychologically, not physically.
  • Dark Nights: Metal (2017-18) - The Dark Knights Batman + Justice League powers + nightmare logic: The Batman Who Laughs, The Red Death, The Dawnbreaker, The Drowned, The Murder Machine, The Merciless, The Devastator.
  • The Flash: Lightning Strikes Twice (2016) Godspeed. August Heart, Barry's friend, becomes a speedster who kills criminals.
  • Aquaman: Black Manta Rising (2017) Black Manta destroys everything Arthur loves.

POST-EVENT STATUS QUO

  • The Source Wall is broken. The multiverse is exposed to the void beyond.
  • The Dark Multiverse exists. Nightmare realities can breach into the main universe.
  • The Batman Who Laughs is loose in the main DCU.
  • Barbatos is defeated but the damage to reality is done.
  • No Justice sets up new team structures.
