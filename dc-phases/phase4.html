Phase 4: The Blackest Night

Years: 2008 - 2011 Anchor Events: Blackest Night / Flashpoint

Death rises. Every dead hero and villain returns as a Black Lantern. Nothing stays buried in the DCU.

0 / 0 completed

EVENT PAUSE - READ FIRST

Blackest Night (2009-10)

What it is: Nekron, lord of the dead, unleashes Black Lantern rings on the deceased. The dead attack the living (temporarily as zombies, some permanently resurrected). The Corps must unite against death itself.

Why it matters: The emotional spectrum is fully revealed. Dead heroes return. Sets up Brightest Day.

Core reading: Blackest Night #0-8, Blackest Night: Batman, Blackest Night: The Flash, Blackest Night: Titans, Green Lantern #43-52.

Flashpoint (2011)

What it is: Barry Allen wakes up in a timeline where his mother never died. Thomas Wayne is Batman. The world is at war.

Why it matters: Barry fixes the timeline but Pandora "merges" three universes (DC, Wildstorm, Vertigo) creating the New 52. The Post-Crisis era ends here.

Core reading: Flashpoint #1-5, Flashpoint: Batman - Knight of Vengeance, Flashpoint: The World of Flashpoint tie-ins for your tracks.

CONCURRENT READING TRACKS

Bat-Family Track

🦇
  • Batman: Battle for the Cowl (2009) Bruce is dead. Dick Grayson vs. Jason Todd vs. Tim Drake for the mantle. Dick becomes Batman.
  • Batman & Robin by Grant Morrison (2009-11) Dick and Damian as Batman and Robin. The best dynamic in Gotham.
  • Batman: The Black Mirror (Detective Comics #871-881, 2011) Dick Batman vs. a villain who knows Gotham's secrets. James Gordon Jr. is a serial killer.
  • Batman Incorporated (2010-11) Bruce returns and goes global with Batman franchises.
  • Red Robin by Christopher Yost (2009-11) Tim Drake operates solo, believing Bruce is alive.
  • Batgirl (Stephanie Brown) (2009-11) Stephanie returns from the dead and becomes Batgirl under Barbara's guidance.
  • Birds of Prey by Gail Simone (2010-11) The team reforms with Black Canary, Huntress, Oracle, and newcomer Hawk and Dove.
  • Gotham City Sirens (2009-11) Catwoman, Harley Quinn, and Poison Ivy as an uneasy alliance.

Flash Family Track

⚡
  • The Flash: Rebirth (2009) Barry Allen returns from the dead. The Speed Force is redefined.
  • The Flash: The Dastardly Death of the Rogues (The Flash #1-6, 2010-11) Barry vs. future Rogues.
  • The Flash: Road to Flashpoint (The Flash #9-12, 2011) Barry discovers his mother's killer was a speedster.

Lantern Track

💚
  • Green Lantern: Blackest Night (2009-10) Hal Jordan leads the war against the Black Lanterns.
  • Green Lantern: Brightest Day (2010-11) 12 heroes and villains are resurrected. What do they do with their second chance?
  • Green Lantern: War of the Green Lanterns (2011) Krona returns and enslaves the Guardians. Hal, Guy, John, and Kyle must fight their own Corps.

Superman Track

🔴
  • Superman: New Krypton (2008-09) 100,000 Kryptonians are freed from Brainiac's bottle. They establish a planet opposite Earth.
  • Superman: World of New Krypton (2009) Superman lives among his people. Zod leads the Kryptonian military.
  • Superman: Last Stand of New Krypton (2010) Brainiac attacks New Krypton. Supergirl's mother Alura dies.
  • Superman: War of the Supermen (2010) Lex Luthor destroys New Krypton. Superman is the last Kryptonian again (except Kara and the Phantom Zone criminals).
  • Superman: Grounded (2010-11) Superman walks across America to reconnect with humanity.
  • Supergirl by Sterling Gates (2008-11) Kara deals with New Krypton's rise and fall. Her mother's death. Her place on Earth.

Wonder Woman Track

⭐
  • Wonder Woman: Rise of the Olympian (2008-09) A new champion created by the gods. Diana vs. Achilles.
  • Wonder Woman: Warkiller (2009) Diana vs. the Olympian gods in open war.
  • Wonder Woman: Odyssey (2010-11) A rewritten timeline where Diana was raised away from Themyscira.

Titans / Justice League Track

🌊
  • Justice League: Generation Lost (2010-11) Booster Gold, Captain Atom, Fire, and Ice try to prove Max Lord is alive and evil. The world has forgotten him.
  • Titans: Villains for Hire (2010) Deathstroke forms a team of villains as mercenaries.
  • Teen Titans by J.T. Krul (2010-11) Damian Wayne joins. Tim Drake leaves.

Cosmic / New Gods Track

🌌
  • Final Crisis Aftermath: Escape (2009) The remnants of the New Gods in a post-Final Crisis world.
  • Brightest Day (2010-11) Martian Manhunter, Hawkman, Hawkgirl, and others are resurrected. Aquaman returns.

Magic / Vertigo Track

🔮
  • Zatanna by Paul Dini (2010-11) Zatanna's first major solo series. Stage magic meets real sorcery.
  • Justice League Dark setup (2011) The magic characters begin clustering for the New 52 launch.

Archers / Street Track

🏹
  • Green Arrow by J.T. Krul (2010-11) Ollie is resurrected post-Blackest Night. Star City is destroyed and rebuilt as a forest.
  • The Question by Denny O'Neil (2010) Renee Montoya's ongoing series.

Martial Arts Track

🥋
  • Batman Incorporated: The Island of Mister Mayhew (2010) Batman's international agents include martial artists from every tradition.
  • Red Hood: The Lost Days (2010) Jason Todd's training across the globe with the All-Caste, a secret martial-arts cult.

Atlantis Track

🌊
  • Brightest Day: Aquaman (2010) Arthur Curry is resurrected. Mera's secret origin is revealed - she is from a dimension of water sorcerers.
  • Aquaman: Brightest Day (2010-11) Arthur struggles with his return and Mera's secrets.

Villain Track

💀
  • Blackest Night (2009-10) Nekron and the Black Lanterns. Every dead villain returns to torment the living. A horror event.
  • Blackest Night: Batman (2009) Dead Batman villains (including the Graysons) attack Dick and Damian.
  • Blackest Night: The Flash (2009) Reverse-Flash returns as a Black Lantern. Bart Allen and other dead speedsters attack Barry.
  • Secret Six: Danse Macabre (2010) The team faces Black Lanterns of their own dead.
  • Batman: The Black Mirror (2011) James Gordon Jr. as a psychological villain. No costume, just pure human evil.
  • Gotham City Sirens (2009-11) Harley Quinn's descent and partial redemption. Poison Ivy's eco-terrorism. Catwoman's moral code.
  • The Flash: Road to Flashpoint (2011) Professor Zoom (Eobard Thawne) reveals he killed Barry's mother and manipulated his entire life.
  • Green Lantern: War of the Green Lanterns (2011) Krona enslaves the Guardians. The ultimate cosmic villain returns.

POST-EVENT STATUS QUO

  • The universe is rebooted into the New 52. Pandora merges DC, Wildstorm, and Vertigo.
  • Barry Allen is the Flash. Wally West does not exist in the New 52 (initially).
  • Bruce Wayne is Batman. Dick returns to Nightwing.
  • Superman's origin is rewritten. He is younger, no longer married to Lois.
  • The Green Lantern continuity largely survives the reboot (Geoff Johns' run continues uninterrupted).
  • Wonder Woman's origin is rewritten. She is now the daughter of Zeus.
  • Aquaman is alive but his history with Mera is altered.
