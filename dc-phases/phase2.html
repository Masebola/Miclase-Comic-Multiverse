Phase 2: The Rise of Villains

Years: 1994 - 2004 Anchor Event: Identity Crisis

Villains stop being jokes. They start targeting families. Gotham falls apart. The heroes' personal lives become war zones.

0 / 0 completed

EVENT PAUSE - READ FIRST

Identity Crisis (2004)

What it is: Sue Dibny is murdered. The heroes' loved ones are targeted. A dark secret from the Justice League's past is exposed.

Why it matters: Shifts DC from "bright" to "personal horror." Villains become lethal threats to families, not just cities. Deathstroke fights the entire JLA.

Core reading: Identity Crisis #1-7. Tie-ins are minimal; the core series is self-contained.

CONCURRENT READING TRACKS

Read all chosen tracks side-by-side within a phase. Do not finish one track before starting another.

Bat-Family Track

🦇
  • Batman: Contagion / Legacy (1996) Ra's al Ghul unleashes a plague on Gotham. Introduces the "legacy" of the Demon.
  • Batman: Cataclysm (1998) Earthquake destroys Gotham.
  • Batman: No Man's Land (1999-2000) The government abandons Gotham. Gangs and villains carve up the city. Introduces Cassandra Cain (Batgirl).
  • Batman: Officer Down (2001) Commissioner Gordon is shot. Renee Montoya investigates.
  • Batman: Bruce Wayne: Murderer? / Fugitive (2002-03) Bruce is framed for murder. He abandons his civilian identity.
  • Batman: Hush (Batman #608-619, 2002-03) A mystery villain manipulates Batman's entire rogues gallery. Riddler is the secret architect.
  • Batman: War Games (2004) Stephanie Brown (Spoiler) tries to prove herself to Batman. She is tortured and presumed dead by Black Mask.
  • Birds of Prey by Chuck Dixon (1996-2002) Oracle and Black Canary operate as a covert team.
  • Nightwing by Chuck Dixon (1996-2002) Dick Grayson establishes himself in Bludhaven.
  • Batgirl (Cassandra Cain) (Batgirl #1-12, 2000-01) Her early solo run. Defines her as a martial-arts weapon learning to be human.
  • Robin by Jon Lewis (2001-04) Tim Drake balances high school and heroics.

Flash Family Track

⚡
  • The Flash: Dead Heat (The Flash #108-111, 1996) Savitar and the Speed Force cult.
  • The Flash: The Human Race (The Flash #136-141, 1998) Wally races cosmic beings for Earth's survival.
  • The Flash: Wonderland (The Flash #164-169, 2000) Wally enters an altered Keystone City.
  • The Flash: Blood Will Run (The Flash #170-176, 2001) Cicada cult kills Flash's allies.
  • The Flash: Blitz (The Flash #197-200, 2003) Hunter Zolomon becomes Zoom. A villain born from Wally's failure to save him.

Lantern Track

💚
  • Green Lantern (Kyle Rayner) by Ron Marz (1994-2001) Kyle as the last Green Lantern. Introduces his artist background and relationship with Jade.
  • Green Lantern: Circle of Fire (2000) Kyle teams with future/alt Lanterns.
  • Green Lantern: The Final Night (1996) Hal Jordan (Parallax) dies saving Earth. The sun is extinguished.

Superman Track

🔴

Supergirl Rule Still Active

  • Superman: The Wedding Album (1996) Clark marries Lois.
  • Superman: Kingdom Come (1996) Elseworlds-adjacent but essential. Defines the Superman archetype for the next 20 years of DC.
  • Superman: Our Worlds at War (2001) Imperiex destroys worlds. Doomsday returns. A cosmic war.
  • Superman: Ending Battle (2002) Lex Luthor orchestrates a 24-hour assault on Superman's life.
  • Superman: Birthright (2003-04) Updated post-Crisis origin. Lex as Clark's childhood friend.
  • Steel (1994-98) John Henry Irons carries the Superman legacy.

Wonder Woman Track

⭐
  • Wonder Woman by John Byrne (1997-01) Donna Troy's origins are re-explored. Hippolyta takes a more active role.
  • Wonder Woman: The Hiketeia (2002) Wonder Woman vs. Batman over a fugitive. Defines Diana's moral absolutism.
  • Wonder Woman: Down to Earth (2003-04) Greg Rucka's run begins. Political intrigue and Cheetah.

Titans / Justice League Track

🌊
  • JLA by Grant Morrison (1997-2000) The "big seven" League. Cosmic scale.
  • JLA: Tower of Babel (2000) Ra's al Ghul steals Batman's contingency plans to defeat the League.
  • JLA: World War III (2000) Mageddon, a cosmic war machine.
  • Titans (1999-2003) The original New Teen Titans as adults.
  • Young Justice (1998-2003) Tim Drake, Superboy, Impulse (Bart Allen). Lighthearted but important for character dynamics.
  • The Titans/Young Justice: Graduation Day (2003) Donna Troy dies. Leads into the 2003 Teen Titans relaunch.

Cosmic / New Gods Track

🌌
  • Orion by Walt Simonson (2000-02) The New Gods through the lens of Orion's rage.
  • JLA: Rock of Ages (1997) Darkseid wins in a future timeline. The League prevents it.
  • Our Worlds at War (2001) Imperiex War. Cosmic-scale destruction.

Magic / Vertigo Track

🔮
  • The Spectre by John Ostrander (1992-98) Jim Corrigan and later Hal Jordan as the Spectre.
  • Day of Judgment (1999) Heaven and Hell go to war. Hal Jordan becomes the Spectre.
  • Hellblazer: The Family Man (Hellblazer #23-24) Constantine vs. a human serial killer.
  • The Books of Magic by Neil Gaiman (1990-91) Timothy Hunter's origin. Defines magic in the DCU.

Archers / Street Track

🏹
  • Green Arrow by Kevin Smith: Quiver / The Sounds of Violence (2001-02) Oliver Queen is resurrected.
  • The Question (Renee Montoya) Featured heavily in Gotham Central.

Martial Arts Track

🥋
  • Batgirl: Year One (2003) Barbara Gordon's origin as Batgirl (set in past, but published here).
  • Birds of Prey Lady Shiva appears as a recurring antagonist/ally.
  • Richard Dragon (2004) Dixon's series re-establishes Richard Dragon, Bronze Tiger, and Lady Shiva in the modern era.

Atlantis Track

🌊
  • Aquaman by Peter David (1994-2001) The hook-hand era. Aquaman becomes a heavy metal barbarian-king.
  • Tempest (1996-98) Garth's solo evolution.

Villain Track

💀
  • Underworld Unleashed (1995) Neron offers villains their hearts' desires in exchange for their souls. Upgrades many rogues.
  • The Final Night (1996) The Sun-Eater. A cosmic villain that requires hero and villain cooperation.
  • Joker: The Devil's Advocate (1996) Joker on death row. Rare legal thriller take.
  • Batman: Legacy (1996) Ra's al Ghul's global plague. A master villain operating at scale.
  • Joker's Last Laugh (2001) Joker is told he has a terminal tumor. He "Joker-izes" every supervillain in the DCU.
  • Batman: Hush (2002-03) Riddler proves he can out-think Batman by manipulating the entire rogues gallery.
  • Superman: Ending Battle (2002) Lex Luthor's personal, petty war on Superman.
  • The Flash: Blitz (2003) Zoom (Hunter Zolomon) is born. A villain who thinks he's helping Wally by making him "better" through tragedy.
  • Deathstroke: The Hunted (1994) Deathstroke framed for murder. His solo series begins.

POST-EVENT STATUS QUO

  • The Justice League's secret (mind-wiping villains) is out. Trust fractures.
  • Tim Drake's father is murdered. Tim's life is destroyed.
  • Deathstroke is established as a metahuman tactician who can defeat the League single-handedly.
  • The villain community is no longer playing by old rules. The coming phases will be bloodier.
