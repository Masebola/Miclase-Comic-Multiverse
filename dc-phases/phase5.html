Phase 5: The New 52

Years: 2011 - 2016 Anchor Event: DC Rebirth

A hard reboot. Younger heroes. Edgier tone. Some characters erased, others reimagined. The continuity is compressed into a 5-year timeline.

0 / 0 completed

EVENT PAUSE - READ FIRST

Forever Evil (2013-14)

What it is: The Crime Syndicate (evil Justice League from Earth-3) invades and conquers Earth. The villains must save the world because the heroes are dead/trapped.

Why it matters: Establishes the New 52's major villain organizations. Lex Luthor becomes a hero (temporarily). Dick Grayson is outed as Nightwing to the world.

Core reading: Forever Evil #1-7, Forever Evil: Arkham War, Forever Evil: Rogues Rebellion, Forever Evil: Blight.

DC Rebirth (2016)

What it is: Not an event but a line-wide relaunch. Wally West returns from the Speed Force. The New 52 Superman dies. The pre-Flashpoint Superman replaces him. The timeline was stolen.

Why it matters: Soft-reboot that restores pre-Flashpoint elements. Wally West is back. The JSA and Titans history is hinted at returning.

Core reading: DC Universe: Rebirth #1, Batman: Rebirth, Superman: Rebirth, Green Lanterns: Rebirth, The Flash: Rebirth, Titans: Rebirth, Nightwing: Rebirth.

CONCURRENT READING TRACKS

Bat-Family Track

🦇
  • Batman: The Court of Owls / Night of the Owls (Batman #1-11, 2011-12) The Court of Owls is introduced. A secret society that has ruled Gotham for centuries. Talon is their assassin.
  • Batman: Death of the Family (Batman #13-17, 2012-13) Joker returns, believes he knows Batman's identity, attacks the Bat-Family.
  • Batman: Zero Year (Batman #21-33, 2013-14) New 52 Batman origin. Riddler destroys Gotham's infrastructure.
  • Batman: Endgame (Batman #35-40, 2014-15) Joker's "final" battle with Batman. Both are left for dead.
  • Batman: Superheavy (Batman #41-50, 2015-16) Jim Gordon is Batman in a mech suit. Bruce Wayne has no memory.
  • Grayson (2014-16) Dick Grayson as a spy for Spyral. His identity is public, so he goes undercover globally.
  • Batgirl (Barbara Gordon) (2011-16) Barbara is no longer Oracle. She has a neural implant that lets her walk. Her own solo series.
  • Batgirl (Burnside era) (2014-16) Barbara moves to Burnside. Lighter tone, tech-focused.
  • Red Hood and the Outlaws (2011-16) Jason Todd, Arsenal (Roy Harper), and Starfire as a team of anti-heroes.
  • Batwoman by J.H. Williams III (2011-13) Kate Kane's visually stunning solo series. Her conflict with the DEO and her sister Beth (Alice).
  • Harley Quinn by Amanda Conner/Jimmy Palmiotti (2013-16) Harley leaves Joker, moves to Coney Island, becomes an anti-hero.

Flash Family Track

⚡
  • The Flash: Move Forward (The Flash #1-8, 2011-12) Barry Allen's New 52 origin. Mob Rule, the Speed Force storm.
  • The Flash: Rogues Revolution (The Flash #9-17, 2012-13) The Rogues get powers. Captain Cold can manipulate absolute zero.
  • The Flash: Reverse (The Flash #20-25, 2013) Daniel West becomes the Reverse-Flash.
  • The Flash: Out of Time (The Flash #30-35, 2014-15) Wally West returns (briefly, then vanishes again). Future Flash (Barry from 20 years ahead) appears.

Lantern Track

💚
  • Green Lantern: Sinestro (Green Lantern #1-6, 2011-12) Sinestro becomes a Green Lantern again. Hal Jordan is his unwilling partner.
  • Green Lantern: Revenge of the Black Hand (Green Lantern #7-12, 2012) Black Hand returns. The Book of the Black.
  • Green Lantern: The Third Army (Green Lantern #13-16, Annual #1, 2012-13) The Guardians create a new emotionless army.
  • Green Lantern: Wrath of the First Lantern (Green Lantern #17-20, 2013) Volthoom, the First Lantern, escapes.
  • Green Lantern Corps by Peter Tomasi (2011-15) Guy Gardner and John Stewart protect the universe.
  • Red Lanterns by Charles Soule (2013-16) Guy Gardner leads the Red Lanterns. Atrocitus is deposed.
  • Green Lantern: New Guardians (2011-15) Kyle Rayner masters all seven emotional spectrum colors.

Superman Track

🔴
  • Superman: What Price Tomorrow? (Superman #1-8, 2011-12) New 52 Superman. Younger, more aggressive, dating Wonder Woman.
  • Superman: H'El on Earth (2012-13) A Kryptonian survivor claims to be Superman's superior. H'El is a villain who loves Krypton more than life.
  • Superman: Doomed (2014) Doomsday infects Superman. He becomes a Doomsday-Superman hybrid.
  • Superman: Before Truth (2015) Superman's secret identity is exposed to the world.
  • Superman: The Final Days of Superman (2016) New 52 Superman dies. The pre-Flashpoint Superman (married to Lois, with son Jon) replaces him.

Wonder Woman Track

⭐
  • Wonder Woman: Blood / Guts / Iron (Wonder Woman #1-18, 2011-13) Brian Azzarello's run. Diana is Zeus's daughter. The Greek gods are reimagined as grotesque, modern figures.
  • Wonder Woman: War (Wonder Woman #19-23, 2013) The First Born, Zeus's eldest son, returns to claim Olympus.
  • Wonder Woman: The Lies / Year One (2016, Rebirth) Greg Rucka returns. The New 52 origin is revealed as a lie. The true origin is restored.

Titans / Justice League Track

🌊
  • Justice League: Origin (2011-12) The New 52 League forms. Darkseid invades Earth.
  • Justice League: Throne of Atlantis (2012-13) Orm (Ocean Master) attacks the surface world. Aquaman must choose sides.
  • Justice League: The Amazo Virus (2014-15) Lex Luthor joins the Justice League.
  • Justice League: Darkseid War (2015-16) Darkseid dies. The Anti-Monitor returns. The New Gods are reborn as mortals.
  • Teen Titans by Scott Lobdell (2011-14) Red Robin leads a team of young heroes.

Cosmic / New Gods Track

🌌
  • Earth 2 (2012-15) A world where Batman, Superman, and Wonder Woman die fighting Darkseid. New heroes emerge.
  • Earth 2: World's End (2014-15) Earth 2 is destroyed. Survivors flee to Prime Earth.

Magic / Vertigo Track

🔮
  • Justice League Dark (2011-15) John Constantine, Zatanna, Deadman, and others form a magic League.
  • Constantine (2013-15) John Constantine in the main DCU (not Vertigo).
  • Trinity of Sin: The Phantom Stranger (2012-14) The Stranger's origin is revealed. He is Judas Iscariot.

Archers / Street Track

🏹
  • Green Arrow by Jeff Lemire (2013-15) The Outsiders War. Oliver Queen's origin is redefined with a mystical element.
  • Green Arrow by Benjamin Percy (2015-16) Rebirth setup. Ollie faces the Ninth Circle.

Martial Arts Track

🥋
  • Batman Incorporated: Demon Star / Gotham's Most Wanted (2012-13) Batman's global army faces Leviathan (Talia al Ghul's organization).
  • Batwing (2011-14) Lucius Fox's son David is Batwing in Africa.

Atlantis Track

🌊
  • Aquaman: The Trench (Aquaman #1-6, 2011) Aquaman faces deep-sea horrors.
  • Aquaman: The Others (Aquaman #7-13, 2012-13) Arthur's pre-Justice League team. Black Manta kills one of them.
  • Aquaman: Death of a King (Aquaman #17-24, 2013-14) The Dead King, first king of Atlantis, returns.

Villain Track

💀
  • Forever Evil (2013-14) The Crime Syndicate. Ultraman, Owlman, Superwoman, Johnny Quick, Power Ring, Atomica, Deathstorm. Evil versions of the Justice League.
  • Forever Evil: Arkham War (2013-14) Gotham's villains fight for control of the city while the Syndicate rules the world. Bane vs. Scarecrow vs. Penguin.
  • Forever Evil: Rogues Rebellion (2013-14) The Rogues refuse to join the Syndicate and fight back. A villain-hero story.
  • Batman: Death of the Family (2012-13) Joker's most personal attack. He believes he is Batman's truest companion.
  • Batman: Endgame (2014-15) Joker's "final" plan. He poisons Gotham with Joker venom.
  • Batman: The Court of Owls (2011-12) The Court of Owls. A centuries-old conspiracy. Talon as their undead assassin.
  • Justice League: Darkseid War (2015-16) Darkseid's death. The Anti-Monitor's return. Grail (Darkseid's daughter) is introduced.
  • Green Lantern: Wrath of the First Lantern (2013) Volthoom. The first Lantern, fueled by the life-force of every being in the universe.

POST-EVENT STATUS QUO

  • The New 52 is over. DC Rebirth restores pre-Flashpoint continuity.
  • Wally West returns. He is the Flash again (briefly, then Barry resumes).
  • The pre-Flashpoint Superman (married to Lois, father of Jon Kent) is the main Superman.
  • New 52 Superman is dead.
  • Bruce Wayne is Batman. The Bat-Family is partially restored.
  • Green Lantern continuity continues largely uninterrupted.
  • Wonder Woman's origin is corrected back to clay/Amazon heritage.
  • The JSA and Titans history is hinted to be returning.

The definitive Elseworlds of the New 52 era. Superman becomes a dictator.
