Phase 0: Post-Crisis Foundation

1986 – 1994

🔄 The Universe Reboots

Crisis on Infinite Earths rewrote DC continuity. Origins are being rewritten. Your characters are meeting for the first time. Read these in order to understand the post-Crisis DC Universe foundation.

📖 Reading Tracks

🦇 Bat-Family Track

Batman: Year One
Batman #404-407 (1987)
The definitive post-Crisis Batman origin. Essential baseline for understanding Bruce Wayne's journey.
Batman: The Long Halloween
1996
Harvey Dent becomes Two-Face. Dick Grayson becomes Nightwing. Sets the tone for the entire Bat-saga.
Batman: Dark Victory
1999
Continuation of The Long Halloween. Tim Drake deduces Batman's identity and becomes Robin.
Batman: The Killing Joke
1988
The Joker paralyzes Barbara Gordon. She becomes Oracle after this pivotal event.
Batman: A Death in the Family
Batman #426-429 (1988)
The Joker murders Jason Todd. This death echoes through DC continuity for decades.
Batman: Knightfall / Knightquest / KnightsEnd
1993-94
Bane breaks Batman's back. Azrael takes over. Bruce Wayne reclaims the mantle.

🦸 Superman Track

The Man of Steel
1986 (John Byrne)
The definitive post-Crisis Superman origin. This is the blueprint for modern Superman continuity.
Superman: Exile
Superman #28-30, Adventures of Superman #451-453 (1989)
Superman exiles himself to space after executing Kryptonian criminals.
Superman: The Death of Superman / Funeral for a Friend / Reign of the Supermen
1992-93
Doomsday kills Superman. Four replacements appear: Steel, Superboy, Cyborg Superman, and the Eradicator.
Superman: The Return of Superman
1993
The real Superman returns. Steel and Superboy remain in the cast.

⭐ Wonder Woman Track

Wonder Woman: Gods and Mortals
Wonder Woman #1-7 (1987) - George Pérez
Diana's post-Crisis origin. The definitive Wonder Woman reboot by legendary artist George Pérez.
Wonder Woman: The Challenge of Artemis
Wonder Woman #92-100 (1994-95)
Artemis temporarily replaces Diana as Wonder Woman, testing Diana's worthiness.

⚡ Flash Family Track

The Flash: Born to Run
The Flash #62-65 (1992)
Wally West's post-Crisis origin as the Flash. The beginning of the Flash legacy.
The Flash: The Return of Barry Allen
The Flash #73-79 (1993)
Wally confronts the legacy he can never escape. A profound character exploration.
The Flash: Terminal Velocity
The Flash #0, 95-100 (1994)
Wally breaks the speed barrier. Introduces the Speed Force concept to DC lore.

🛡️ Justice League Track

Legends
1986-87
Post-Crisis first team-up. Launches the Suicide Squad and the new Justice League.
Justice League International
1987-89
The "bwa-ha-ha" era. Booster Gold and Blue Beetle (Ted Kord) join a more lighthearted League.
