Phase 1: Post-Crisis Foundation

Years: 1986 - 1994 Anchor Event: Zero Hour: Crisis in Time

The universe has been rebooted. Origins are being rewritten. Your characters are meeting for the first time.

0 / 0 completed

EVENT PAUSE - READ FIRST

Zero Hour: Crisis in Time (1994)

What it is: Hal Jordan (Parallax) tries to rewrite time to fix his broken life. Time itself collapses.

Why it matters: Hard-resets the Post-Crisis timeline. Fixes inconsistencies and establishes the "rolling timeline" DC uses going forward.

Core reading: Zero Hour: Crisis in Time #4-0 (countdown issues), Zero Hour tie-ins for your chosen tracks only.

CONCURRENT READING TRACKS

Read all chosen tracks side-by-side within a phase. Do not finish one track before starting another.

Bat-Family Track

🦇
  • Batman: Year One (Batman #404-407, 1987) The definitive post-Crisis Batman origin. Essential baseline.
  • Batman: The Man Who Laughs (2005, but set early) Post-Crisis first encounter with the Joker. Sets the tone for their entire war.
  • Batman: The Long Halloween / Dark Victory (1996-99) Harvey Dent becomes Two-Face; Dick Grayson becomes Nightwing; origin of the Roman Holiday killer.
  • Batman: The Killing Joke (1988) Joker paralyzes Barbara Gordon. She becomes Oracle after this.
  • Batman: A Death in the Family (Batman #426-429, 1988) Joker murders Jason Todd. This death echoes for decades.
  • Batman: A Lonely Place of Dying (Batman #440-442, New Titans #60-61, 1989) Tim Drake deduces Batman's identity and becomes Robin.
  • Batman: Sword of Azrael (Batman: Sword of Azrael #1-4, 1992) Introduces Jean-Paul Valley (Azrael).
  • Batman: Knightfall / Knightquest / KnightsEnd (1993-94) Bane breaks Batman. Azrael takes over. Bruce Wayne reclaims the mantle.

Flash Family Track

⚡
  • The Flash: Born to Run (The Flash #62-65, 1992) Wally West's post-Crisis origin as Flash.
  • The Flash: The Return of Barry Allen (The Flash #73-79, 1993) Wally confronts the legacy he can never escape.
  • The Flash: Terminal Velocity (The Flash #0, 95-100, 1994) Wally breaks the speed barrier. Introduces the Speed Force concept.

Lantern Track

💚
  • Green Lantern: Emerald Dawn (1989-90) Hal Jordan's post-Crisis origin.
  • Green Lantern: Emerald Dawn II (1991) Hal's training under Sinestro.
  • Green Lantern: Emerald Twilight (Green Lantern #48-50, 1994) Hal Jordan snaps, destroys the Corps, becomes Parallax. Directly leads into Zero Hour.

Superman Track

🔴

Supergirl Rule Active - NO SUPERGIRL

  • The Man of Steel (1986) John Byrne's post-Crisis Superman origin. The blueprint.
  • Superman: Exile (Superman #28-30, Adventures of Superman #451-453, 1989) Superman exiles himself to space after executing Kryptonian criminals.
  • Superman: The Death of Superman / Funeral for a Friend / Reign of the Supermen (1992-93) Doomsday kills Superman. Four replacements appear: Steel, Superboy, Cyborg Superman, and the Eradicator.
  • Superman: The Return of Superman (1993) The real Superman returns. Steel and Superboy remain in the cast.

Wonder Woman Track

⭐
  • Wonder Woman: Gods and Mortals (Wonder Woman #1-7, 1987) George Perez reboot. Diana's post-Crisis origin.
  • Wonder Woman: The Challenge of Artemis (Wonder Woman #92-100, 1994-95) Artemis temporarily replaces Diana as Wonder Woman.

Titans / Justice League Track

🌊
  • Legends (1986-87) Post-Crisis first team-up. Launches the Suicide Squad, new Justice League, and Shazam.
  • Justice League International (1987-89) The "bwa-ha-ha" era. Booster Gold and Blue Beetle (Ted Kord) join.
  • New Teen Titans by Wolfman/Perez (1980-84, pre-Crisis but essential) Read only if you need Dick Grayson/Nightwing, Starfire, Raven, and Donna Troy history. Post-Crisis continuity largely preserves this run.
  • The New Titans (1988-96) Continues the team into the post-Crisis era.

Cosmic / New Gods Track

🌌
  • Cosmic Odyssey (1988) Darkseid and the New Gods team with heroes against the Anti-Life Entity. Establishes Darkseid's cosmic scale.
  • New Gods by John Byrne (1989-91) Post-Crisis New Gods status quo.

Magic / Vertigo Track

🔮
  • Swamp Thing by Alan Moore (Saga of the Swamp Thing #20-64, 1984-87) Redefines Swamp Thing as a plant elemental. Introduces John Constantine.
  • Hellblazer by Jamie Delano (1988-91) Constantine's early, grim horror stories.
  • The Sandman (1989-96) Dream of the Endless. Largely self-contained but informs all magic users in the DCU.

Archers / Street Track

🏹
  • Green Arrow: The Longbow Hunters (1987) Oliver Queen kills for the first time. Black Canary is attacked.
  • The Question by Dennis O'Neil (1987-90) Vic Sage's philosophical martial-arts detective series.
  • Black Canary mini-series (1991-93) Dinah Lance solo work between JLI and Birds of Prey.

Martial Arts Track

🥋
  • Richard Dragon, Kung-Fu Fighter (1970s pre-Crisis, but referenced) Skip unless you want deep cuts; origin of Richard Dragon and Bronze Tiger.
  • Batman: The Last Arkham (Batman: Shadow of the Bat #1-4, 1992) Zsasz vs. Batman in Arkham. Introduces Jeremiah Arkham.

Atlantis Track

🌊
  • Aquaman: Time and Tide (1993-94) Post-Crisis Aquaman origin. Garth becomes Tempest.

Villain Track

💀
  • The Joker: The Devil's Advocate (1996, but set early) Joker on death row. A definitive character study.
  • Batman: Vengeance of Bane (Batman: Vengeance of Bane #1, 1993) Bane's origin. Read before Knightfall.
  • Batman: The Last Arkham (1992) Zsasz's definitive early story.
  • Superman: The Death of Superman (1992) Doomsday's rampage. A villain as a force of nature.
  • Green Lantern: Emerald Twilight (1994) Hero becomes villain. Hal Jordan's fall is one of DC's greatest tragedies.

POST-EVENT STATUS QUO

  • Time is healed but slightly altered. Some origins are softened.
  • Hal Jordan is Parallax and will remain a villain/antagonist for a decade.
  • Kyle Rayner becomes the last Green Lantern (introduced right after this phase).
  • Bat-Family is intact: Bruce is Batman, Dick is Nightwing, Tim is Robin, Barbara is Oracle, Jason is dead.
  • Superman is alive; Steel and Superboy are active.
