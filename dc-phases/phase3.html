Phase 3: The Crisis Loop

Years: 2004 - 2008 Anchor Event: Final Crisis

The multiverse returns. Crisis upon crisis reshapes reality. Batman "dies." Darkseid falls. Nothing will ever be the same.

0 / 0 completed

EVENT PAUSE - READ FIRST

Infinite Crisis (2005-06)

What it is: The multiverse briefly returns. Superboy-Prime and Alexander Luthor try to create a "perfect" Earth. Superboy (Conner Kent) dies. Donna Troy returns.

Why it matters: The original Crisis survivors return as villains. The multiverse is reborn as 52 universes.

Core reading: Infinite Crisis #1-7, Countdown to Infinite Crisis, The OMAC Project, Villains United, Day of Vengeance, Rann-Thanagar War.

Final Crisis (2008)

What it is: Darkseid wins. He conquers Earth using the Anti-Life Equation. Batman confronts Darkseid and is hit by the Omega Sanction, sending him through time.

Why it matters: The New Gods die. Batman "dies." The multiverse is fundamentally changed. Superman sings Darkseid to death.

Core reading: Final Crisis #1-7, Batman R.I.P., Final Crisis: Superman Beyond, Final Crisis: Submit.

CONCURRENT READING TRACKS

Read all chosen tracks side-by-side within a phase. Do not finish one track before starting another.

Bat-Family Track

🦇
  • Batman: Under the Hood (2005) Jason Todd as Red Hood. A villain who knows Batman's every move.
  • Batman: Face the Face (2006) One Year Later. Batman returns after Infinite Crisis.
  • Batman and Son (2006) Grant Morrison's run begins. Damian Wayne is introduced as Bruce's son with Talia al Ghul.
  • Batman: The Black Glove (2007) The Club of Villains. Dr. Hurt begins his assault on Bruce Wayne's sanity.
  • Batman R.I.P. (2008) The Black Glove's final assault. Dr. Hurt claims to be Thomas Wayne. Bruce is broken but survives through a backup personality.
  • The Resurrection of Ra's al Ghul (2007) Ra's al Ghul cheats death again. The Demon's Head is eternal.
  • Nightwing: One Year Later (2006-08) Dick Grayson in New York, then back to Bludhaven.
  • Robin: One Year Later (2006-08) Tim Drake deals with his father's death and Damian's arrival.

Flash Family Track

⚡
  • The Flash: Rogue War (2005) The Rogues split into factions. Captain Cold vs. the Top.
  • The Flash: Full Throttle (2006-07) The Rogues kill Bart Allen. A villain act with permanent consequences.
  • Final Crisis: Rogues' Revenge (2008) The Rogues reject Libra's villain army. A villain story about villain integrity.

Lantern Track

💚
  • Green Lantern: Rebirth (2004-05) Hal Jordan returns from the dead. Parallax is revealed to be a fear entity that possessed him.
  • Green Lantern: No Fear (2005-06) Hal re-establishes himself. Hector Hammond and the Manhunters return.
  • Green Lantern: Sinestro Corps War (2007) Sinestro as a general. Anti-Monitor as a weapon. The yellow rings spread fear across the universe.
  • Green Lantern: Rage of the Red Lanterns (2008) Atrocitus and the Red Lantern Corps debut. The emotional spectrum expands.

Superman Track

🔴

Supergirl Rule Now Active - Kara Zor-El appears

  • Superman: For Tomorrow (Superman #204-215, 2004-05) Superman deals with the disappearance of 1 million people. A meditation on power and failure.
  • Superman: Up, Up, and Away (Superman #650-653, 2006) Post-Infinite Crisis. Clark regains his powers after a year depowered.
  • Superman: Last Son (Action Comics #844-846, 851, Annual #11, 2006-08) General Zod and other Phantom Zone criminals escape. Introduces Chris Kent (adopted Kryptonian son).
  • Superman: Brainiac (Action Comics #866-870, 2008) Brainiac returns. Kandor is restored. A defining modern Superman story.
  • Supergirl by Jeph Loeb (Supergirl #1-5, 2005) Kara Zor-El's first solo series post-entry. She struggles with Kryptonian heritage and Earth's expectations.
  • Supergirl by Sterling Gates (2008-11) Begins here and continues into Phase 4. Kara finds her place.

Wonder Woman Track

⭐
  • Wonder Woman: The Circle (Wonder Woman #14-17, 2008) Gail Simone's opening arc. Diana vs. her mother's Amazon rivals.
  • Wonder Woman: Ends of the Earth (Wonder Woman #20-22, 2008) Diana vs. the gods themselves.

Titans / Justice League Track

🌊
  • 52 (2006-07) The missing year after Infinite Crisis. Booster Gold's arc is essential. The Question (Vic Sage) passes the mantle to Renee Montoya.
  • One Year Later (2006) All titles jump forward 12 months post-Infinite Crisis.
  • Justice League of America by Brad Meltzer (2006-07) The League reforms with a conspiracy-mystery structure.
  • Teen Titans by Geoff Johns (2003-07) Tim Drake leads the team. Conner Kent's death and return.
  • Blue Beetle (Jaime Reyes) (2006-09) The scarab bonds with Jaime. A new legacy hero.

Cosmic / New Gods Track

🌌
  • Seven Soldiers: Mister Miracle (2005-06) Grant Morrison's reimagining. Shilo Norman escapes the Omega Sanction.
  • Death of the New Gods (2007-08) The New Gods are murdered one by one. Leads directly into Final Crisis.

Magic / Vertigo Track

🔮
  • Shadowpact (2006-08) A team of magic users including Zatanna, Detective Chimp, and Nightshade. The Spectre goes rogue.
  • The Spectre: Crispus Allen (2006-08) A new host for the Spectre. Divine vengeance in a corrupt world.

Archers / Street Track

🏹
  • Green Arrow: Heading into the Light (2005-06) Oliver Queen becomes mayor of Star City.
  • Black Canary: Wedding Planner (2007) Dinah and Ollie's wedding.
  • The Question: 52 (2006-07) Renee Montoya trains under Richard Dragon and takes up the mask.

Martial Arts Track

🥋
  • Batman: Death and the Maidens (2003-04) Ra's al Ghul's daughter Nyssa and his final death (temporary).
  • Lady Shiva: Deathstroke crossover (2005) Shiva and Deathstroke clash over Cassandra Cain.

Atlantis Track

🌊
  • Aquaman: Sword of Atlantis (2006-07) Arthur Joseph Curry becomes the new Aquaman after Orin's disappearance.
  • Aquaman: The Dweller in the Depths (2007) Orin returns, transformed.

Villain Track

💀
  • Infinite Crisis (2005-06) Superboy-Prime as a villain who thinks he's a hero. Alexander Luthor Jr.'s cosmic-scale manipulation.
  • Villains United (2005) Lex Luthor's Secret Society of Super-Villains vs. the Secret Six. Catman's redemption arc begins.
  • Secret Six by Gail Simone (2006-08) Deadshot, Catman, Scandal Savage, Ragdoll, and more. The best villain team book DC has ever published.
  • Green Lantern: Sinestro Corps War (2007) Sinestro as a general. Anti-Monitor as a weapon. The yellow rings spread fear across the universe.
  • Batman: Under the Hood (2005) Jason Todd as Red Hood. A villain who knows Batman's every move.
  • The Resurrection of Ra's al Ghul (2007) Ra's al Ghul cheats death again. The Demon's Head is eternal.
  • Final Crisis: Rogues' Revenge (2008) The Rogues reject Libra's villain army. A villain story about villain integrity.

POST-EVENT STATUS QUO

  • Batman is "dead" (sent through time by Darkseid's Omega Sanction). Dick Grayson will become Batman.
  • The New Gods are dead (mostly). Darkseid is defeated but the Fourth World is shattered.
  • The multiverse exists again but as 52 distinct universes, not infinite.
  • Hal Jordan is the Green Lantern of Earth. Kyle, Guy, and John are Honor Guard.
  • Wally West is missing in the Speed Force with his family.
  • Supergirl (Kara Zor-El) is established as a hero but still struggling with her place.
  • The Rogues have killed a Flash and are now pariahs even among villains.
