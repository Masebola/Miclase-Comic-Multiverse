How to Use This Guide

Everything you need to know to navigate the cosmic multiverse of comics

🎯 Getting Started

  1. Choose Your Universe: Start with either the DC Universe or Marvel Universe roadmap.
  2. Pick Your Tracks: You do NOT need to read every track. Choose based on your favorite characters using the Character Roster.
  3. Start from Phase 0 or Jump In: Phase 0 covers the foundations, but you can start at any phase that interests you.
  4. Read Side-by-Side: Within a phase, read all your chosen tracks concurrently. Don't finish one track before starting another.

⛔ Understanding Event Pauses

When you see an Event Pause banner, it means:

  • Stop all your current reading tracks
  • Read the specified event (e.g., Crisis on Infinite Earths, Civil War)
  • Read any status quo updates mentioned
  • Then resume your chosen tracks with the new context

Events change the universe! Reading them in order ensures you understand why things are different afterward.

🏷️ Understanding Tags

Essential

Must-read stories that define the character or era.

Major Event

Universe-changing crossovers that affect multiple characters.

Optional

Good stories you can skip without losing continuity.

Cosmic

Space-based stories with cosmic-level stakes.

✓ Tracking Your Progress

  1. Check Off Comics: Click the checkbox next to any comic to mark it as read.
  2. Progress is Saved: Your reading progress is saved in your browser's local storage.
  3. View Progress: The progress bar on each page shows how far you've come.
  4. Clear Progress: To reset your progress, clear your browser's local storage for this site.

🔍 Using Search & Filters

  • Search: Use the search box to find specific comics, characters, or storylines.
  • Filters: Click filter buttons to show only specific character tracks (Batman, Spider-Man, X-Men, etc.).
  • Expand Phases: Click on any phase header to expand or collapse its contents.

📚 Reading Order Philosophy

These roadmaps follow several principles:

  • Publication Order Over Story Order: Generally, read comics in the order they were published, not internal chronology.
  • Events as Anchor Points: Major events serve as synchronization points between different character tracks.
  • Quality Over Completeness: We prioritize great runs over complete continuity. You don't need to read every issue.
  • Flexibility: These are guides, not rules. Skip what doesn't interest you.

🌍 Elseworlds & What-Ifs

Stories in the Elseworlds section can be read anytime:

  • They exist outside main continuity
  • No prerequisites required
  • Great palate cleansers between heavy reading
  • Some are the best stories ever told in comics

💡 Tips for New Readers

  • Start with Essentials: Check the Essential Reads page for curated must-reads.
  • Pick One Character: If overwhelmed, follow just one character's track through all phases.
  • Don't Fear Confusion: Some confusion is normal. Context will build over time.
  • Use Wikipedia: If you're lost, a quick wiki search can fill gaps without spoiling too much.
  • Enjoy the Journey: Comics are meant to be fun. Skip what bores you, linger on what excites you.

📖 Where to Read

Comics can be found through:

  • Marvel Unlimited: Subscription service with most Marvel comics.
  • DC Universe Infinite: Subscription service with most DC comics.
  • Comixology: Digital purchase of individual issues and trades.
  • Local Comic Shop: Support your LCS for physical copies!
  • Library: Many libraries carry graphic novels and have digital comic apps.
  • Collected Editions: Trade paperbacks and omnibuses collect story arcs.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need to read everything?

A: Absolutely not! Pick your favorite characters and follow their tracks. Skip anything that doesn't interest you.

Q: Can I start with modern comics?

A: Yes! Many modern runs are designed as jumping-on points. Phase 4+ in both universes works great for new readers.

Q: What if I can't find a specific comic?

A: Most comics on these lists are collected in trades or available digitally. If you can't find one, skip it and move on.

Q: Should I read DC or Marvel first?

A: Whichever characters interest you more! Both universes stand alone. You can read them simultaneously or one at a time.
