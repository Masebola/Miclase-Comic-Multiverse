Pre-Crisis Foundation

1938 - 1985

⚡ Foundation Era

The Golden and Silver Age stories that established DC's greatest heroes and the original multiverse.

🦇 Batman Foundations

Detective Comics #38 (1940)
Dick Grayson debuts as Robin - The first sidekick
Batman #1 (1940)
Catwoman and Joker debut
Batman: Strange Apparitions
Detective Comics #469-476, 478-479 - Bronze Age modernization

🦸 Superman Foundations

Action Comics #1 (1938)
Superman and Lois Lane debut
Action Comics #252 (1959)
Supergirl (Kara Zor-El) debuts
Superman: Kryptonite Nevermore
Superman #233-238, 240-242 - Bronze Age story

⚡ Flash Foundations

Showcase #4 (1956)
Barry Allen debuts as The Flash
The Flash #123 (1961)
"Flash of Two Worlds" - Jay Garrick appears

🪐 Cosmic Foundations

Jack Kirby's Fourth World Omnibus
New Gods #1-11, Forever People #1-11, Mister Miracle #1-18
Introduction of Darkseid, Orion, the Source, and the Anti-Life Equation

Post-Crisis Foundation

1986 - 1994

⛔ EVENT PAUSE - Read First

Crisis on Infinite Earths #1-12 - The Anti-Monitor destroys countless Earths. Barry Allen dies. Supergirl dies. The multiverse becomes a single universe.

🦇 Bat-Family Track

Batman: Year One
Batman #404-407 (1987) by Frank Miller
The definitive post-Crisis Batman origin. Essential baseline.
Essential
Batman: The Man Who Laughs
Ed Brubaker & Doug Mahnke
Post-Crisis first encounter with the Joker.
Batman: The Killing Joke
Alan Moore & Brian Bolland (1988)
The Joker shoots Barbara Gordon, paralyzing her. She becomes Oracle.
Essential
Batman: A Death in the Family
Batman #426-429 by Jim Starlin
Jason Todd dies. The Joker murders him. This death echoes for decades.
Essential
Batman: A Lonely Place of Dying
Batman #440-442, New Titans #60-61
Tim Drake deduces Batman's identity and becomes Robin.
Batman: Knightfall Saga
Knightfall / Knightquest / KnightsEnd (1993-94)
Bane breaks Batman. Azrael takes over. Bruce Wayne reclaims the mantle.
Major Event

🦸 Superman Track

The Man of Steel
Man of Steel #1-6 (1986) by John Byrne
Post-Crisis Superman origin. The blueprint.
Essential
The Death of Superman / Reign of the Supermen
1992-93 Crossover Event
Doomsday kills Superman. Four replacements appear: Steel, Superboy, Cyborg Superman, and the Eradicator.
Major Event

⚡ Flash Track

The Flash: Born to Run
The Flash #62-65 (1992)
Wally West's post-Crisis origin as Flash.
The Flash: Terminal Velocity
The Flash #0, 95-100 (1994)
Wally breaks the speed barrier. Introduces the Speed Force concept.
Essential

💚 Green Lantern Track

Green Lantern: Emerald Dawn I & II
1989-1991
Hal Jordan's post-Crisis origin and training under Sinestro.
Green Lantern: Emerald Twilight
Green Lantern #48-50 (1994)
Hal Jordan snaps, destroys the Corps, becomes Parallax. Leads into Zero Hour.
Essential
2

Rise of Villains

1994 - 2004

⛔ EVENT PAUSE - Read First

Identity Crisis #1-7 - Sue Dibny is murdered. A dark secret from the Justice League's past is exposed. Shifts DC from "bright" to "personal horror."

🦇 Bat-Family Track

Batman: The Long Halloween
Jeph Loeb & Tim Sale (1996-97)
13-issue mystery spanning Batman's early career. Harvey Dent becomes Two-Face.
Essential
Batman: Dark Victory
Jeph Loeb & Tim Sale
Direct sequel. Introduces Dick Grayson as Robin in the modern era.
Batman: No Man's Land
1999-2000 Crossover
The government abandons Gotham. Introduces Cassandra Cain (Batgirl).
Major Event
Batman: Hush
Batman #608-619 by Jeph Loeb & Jim Lee
A mystery villain manipulates Batman's entire rogues gallery.
Essential

⚖️ Justice League Track

JLA by Grant Morrison
JLA #1-41 (1997-2000)
The "big seven" League. Cosmic scale. Definitive modern League run.
Essential
JLA: Tower of Babel
JLA #43-46 by Mark Waid
Ra's al Ghul steals Batman's contingency plans to defeat the League.
Essential
3

Crisis Loop

2004 - 2008

⛔ EVENT PAUSE - Read First

Infinite Crisis #1-7 - The multiverse briefly returns. Superboy-Prime and Alexander Luthor try to create a "perfect" Earth. Conner Kent (Superboy) dies.

🌌 Core Events

52
52-issue weekly series (2006-07)
The missing year after Infinite Crisis. Booster Gold's arc. Vic Sage passes the Question mantle to Renee Montoya.
Major Event
Final Crisis
Grant Morrison (2008)
Darkseid conquers Earth. Batman is "killed" by Omega Sanction. The New Gods die.
Major Event

🦇 Bat-Family Track

Batman: Under the Hood
Batman #635-641, 645-650 by Judd Winick
Jason Todd returns as Red Hood. The true resurrection.
Essential
Batman & Son
Batman #655-658, 663-675 by Grant Morrison
Damian Wayne debuts.
Batman: R.I.P.
Batman #676-683 by Grant Morrison
The Black Glove finally seems to break Batman.
Essential

💚 Green Lantern Track

Green Lantern: Rebirth
Geoff Johns (2004-05)
Hal Jordan returns. The Spectre/Hal entity is resolved. Parallax revealed as fear entity.
Essential
Sinestro Corps War
2007
Sinestro as a general. Anti-Monitor as a weapon. The yellow rings spread fear across the universe.
Major Event
4

Blackest Night

2008 - 2011

⛔ EVENT PAUSE - Read First

Blackest Night - Nekron, lord of the dead, unleashes Black Lantern rings on the deceased. The dead attack the living. Every Lantern Corps must unite.

💚 Green Lantern Track

Blackest Night
2009-10
The emotional spectrum war. All seven Lantern Corps unite against the Black Lanterns.
Major Event
Brightest Day
2010-11
Twelve heroes and villains are resurrected. The White Lantern appears.

🦇 Bat-Family Track

Batman: Battle for the Cowl
2009
With Bruce gone, Dick, Jason, and others fight to become the new Batman.
Batman & Robin by Grant Morrison
Batman and Robin #1-16
Dick Grayson as Batman with Damian Wayne as Robin.
Essential
The Return of Bruce Wayne
Grant Morrison (2010)
Bruce travels through time to return to the present.

The New 52

2011 - 2016

⛔ EVENT PAUSE - Read First

Flashpoint #1-5 - Barry Allen creates an alternate timeline. Thomas Wayne is Batman. The universe is reset. The New 52 begins.

🦇 Batman Track

Batman: Court of Owls
Batman #1-11 by Scott Snyder
A secret society has controlled Gotham for centuries. One of the best modern Batman runs.
Essential
Batman: Death of the Family
Batman #13-17 by Scott Snyder
The Joker returns with his face cut off, targeting the entire Bat-Family.
Essential
Batman: Endgame
Batman #35-40 by Scott Snyder
The Joker's ultimate plan. Batman and Joker's "final" confrontation.

⚖️ Justice League Track

Justice League: Origin
Justice League #1-6 by Geoff Johns & Jim Lee
The New 52 Justice League forms to fight Darkseid.
Forever Evil
2013-14
The Crime Syndicate conquers Earth. Lex Luthor leads the villains against them.
Major Event

DC Universe: Rebirth #1 - Wally West returns. The Watchmen connection is revealed. Legacy heroes return. The DC Universe remembers what was lost.

🌌 Core Events

Dark Nights: Metal
Scott Snyder (2017-18)
The Dark Multiverse. The Batman Who Laughs debuts. Barbatos invades.
Major Event
Doomsday Clock
Geoff Johns (2017-19)
Superman vs. Doctor Manhattan. The Watchmen meet the DC Universe.
Essential

🦇 Batman Track

Batman by Tom King
Batman #1-85
Epic run featuring Batman and Catwoman's relationship, Bane's master plan.
Essential

Death & Doom

2020 - 2022

Dark Nights: Death Metal - The Batman Who Laughs becomes the Darkest Knight. Wonder Woman leads the charge. The multiverse is remade as the Omniverse.

🌌 Core Events

Dark Nights: Death Metal
Scott Snyder (2020-21)
The finale of the Metal saga. Everything matters. The Omniverse is born.
